# Governance

The project is young and small. This document describes how decisions are made today, and how that will evolve.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, publish releases and take final decisions. Current maintainer: [@Mister-iks](https://github.com/Mister-iks).
- **Content reviewers** check that labs and techniques are technically correct and pedagogically clear.
- **Language coordinators** keep a translation consistent and decide when it is ready to publish.

Anyone who contributes regularly and constructively can be invited to one of these roles. The goal is to have at least one co-maintainer before v0.3, so the project never depends on a single person.

## Decisions

- Day to day changes: through pull requests, merged by a maintainer after review.
- Changes to the lab format, the engine model or the product principles: through an issue labeled `rfc`, open for comments for at least 7 days before a decision.
- Disagreements are resolved by discussion first; if needed, maintainers decide and explain why.

## Principles that do not change

- The project stays simulation only: no feature that contacts, scans or attacks real systems.
- The core stays free, open source and usable without an account.
- Accessibility and support for many languages are requirements, not extras.
